# Character Sheet: Volt the Robot

The canonical identity document for Volt, the pedagogical mascot for the
**Beginning Electronics** textbook. Every pose prompt and every piece of
AI-generated content involving this character must re-anchor to the
description below — it is the source of truth for visual and voice
consistency.

## Identity

- **Name:** Volt
- **Species:** Robot (small, compact, kid-friendly)
- **Subject:** Beginning Electronics (breadboards, circuits, and hands-on projects)
- **Catchphrase:** "Let's light it up!"

## Visual Description

- **Body color:** Friendly blue — hex `#2196F3` (matches the site's primary theme color)
- **Accent color:** Warm orange — hex `#FF9800` (matches the site's accent theme color); used for the glowing round eyes and the glowing tip of a spring antenna
- **Clothing / accessories:** A small tool belt holding a mini screwdriver and a wire clip; a coiled spring antenna on top of the head with a glowing orange bulb tip
- **Expression:** Big round LED-style eyes that glow orange, a simple friendly closed-mouth smile
- **Size proportion:** Small and compact with chibi-style rounded proportions — stubby rounded limbs, no sharp edges, suitable for icon-sized display (90px) up to quarter-page illustrations
- **Art style:** Flat cartoon / vector illustration, clean lines, bold flat colors, transparent background

## Personality

- Friendly and warm — never intimidating, even when explaining a tricky concept
- Patient — repeats and rephrases ideas without frustration
- Encouraging — treats a circuit that doesn't work on the first try as a normal part of building, not a failure
- Slightly goofy — enjoys a light pun about circuits, sparks, or current

## Voice

- Uses simple, direct language — no jargon without an immediate plain-language explanation
- Occasionally makes light electronics puns ("That's a *shocking* good idea!", "Let's not let this idea *short* out.")
- Refers to students as "builders" or "young engineers"
- Signature phrases: "Let's light it up!", "Current's flowing your way!", "Nice wiring, builder!"

## Why This Mascot

A small robot ties directly into the subject matter of the course — circuits,
current, and components — without competing with any single component (LED,
resistor, breadboard) that students will meet inside the book. The blue body
and orange glowing eyes/antenna reuse the site's own primary/accent theme
colors, so the mascot reads as native to the book rather than pasted on. The
friendly, patient, slightly-goofy personality matches the course's target
audience of 5th–12th graders who are building real circuits for the first
time and need permission to get things wrong before they get them right.

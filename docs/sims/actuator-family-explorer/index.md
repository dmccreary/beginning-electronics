## About This MicroSim

This interactive infographic compares four electromagnetic output devices by the kind of motion or switching they provide and the driver circuitry they need.

## How to Use

1. In **Explore** mode, click a column—or focus it with the keyboard and press Enter—to reveal a description and selection guidance.
2. Select **Quiz Me** and answer each use-case question by choosing the matching image.
3. Compare at least two columns and explain which visible and electrical differences matter most.

## Compared Items

1. DC motor
2. Electromechanical relay
3. Push-pull solenoid
4. Hobby servo

## Lesson Plan

### Grade Level

Grades 6–10; beginning electronics

### Duration

10–15 minutes

### Learning Objective

Students will match continuous rotation, isolated switching, linear motion, and commanded position to the correct actuator.

### Activities

1. **Explore:** Inspect each column and identify a visible clue.
2. **Compare:** Contrast two choices using the detail panel.
3. **Apply:** Complete the quiz and justify each selection aloud or in writing.

### Assessment

Ask learners to choose an actuator for a wheel, isolated mains control, door latch, and steering linkage, including required protection.

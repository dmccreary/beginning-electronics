## About This MicroSim

This interactive infographic connects the physical appearance of three common capacitor families to polarity, typical capacitance, and practical circuit roles.

## How to Use

1. In **Explore** mode, click a column—or focus it with the keyboard and press Enter—to reveal a description and selection guidance.
2. Select **Quiz Me** and answer each use-case question by choosing the matching image.
3. Compare at least two columns and explain which visible and electrical differences matter most.

## Compared Items

1. Ceramic capacitor

## Lesson Plan

### Grade Level

Grades 6–10; beginning electronics

### Duration

10–15 minutes

### Learning Objective

Students will explain how polarity, construction, and typical capacitance make each capacitor family suitable for different uses.

### Activities

1. **Explore:** Inspect each column and identify a visible clue.
2. **Compare:** Contrast two choices using the detail panel.
3. **Apply:** Complete the quiz and justify each selection aloud or in writing.

### Assessment

Choose a capacitor for high-frequency bypassing, bulk power smoothing, and compact stable decoupling, then justify each selection.

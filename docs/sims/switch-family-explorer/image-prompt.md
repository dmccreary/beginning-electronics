Composition/framing: extra-wide landscape, straight-on five-column comparison, one switch per column, equal column widths, no overlaps, all objects fully visible with margin around them.

Lighting/mood: soft neutral studio lighting, approachable and precise.

Color palette: slate blue, teal, amber, coral, and violet panel accents; switches retain realistic material colors.

Constraints: absolutely NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO labels, NO title, NO arrows, NO callout marks, NO watermark, NO logos. Exactly five switch objects, in the stated left-to-right order. Keep each switch isolated within its column.

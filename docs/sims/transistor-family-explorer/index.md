## About This MicroSim

This interactive infographic compares five widely encountered bipolar junction transistors, ranging from small-signal NPN and PNP devices to the much larger 2N3055 power transistor.

## How to Use

1. In **Explore** mode, click a column—or focus it with the keyboard and press Enter—to reveal a description and selection guidance.
2. Select **Quiz Me** and answer each use-case question by choosing the matching image.
3. Compare at least two columns and explain which visible and electrical differences matter most.

## Lesson Plan

### Grade Level

Grades 6–10; beginning electronics

### Duration

10–15 minutes

### Learning Objective

Students will compare BJT polarity, package, voltage, current, thermal needs, and likely application while recognizing that exact pinouts require a manufacturer datasheet.

### Activities

1. **Explore:** Inspect each column and identify a visible clue.
2. **Compare:** Contrast two choices using the detail panel.
3. **Apply:** Complete the quiz and justify each selection aloud or in writing.

### Assessment

Ask learners to choose a transistor for a small signal, a moderate relay load, a high-side switch, and a power stage, then name the datasheet facts they must verify.
